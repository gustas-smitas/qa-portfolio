## What I learned today

- learned about the difference between static testing and dynamic testing. Car analogy works well for that: static is inspecting under the hood and reading the service history, dynamic is actually going for a test drive. Static finds defects directly and dynamic find failures then backtracks to bugs.
- learned about the review types: the informal, walkthrough, technical review and inspection and that they are risk based - the more risks missed bugs could bring the more thorough the review needs to be.
- learned that reviews need to have success factors like clear exit criteria, small chunks, prep time, safe environment and management support
